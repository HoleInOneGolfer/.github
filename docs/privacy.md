# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** May 12, 2026

## 1. The Big Picture
I’m just one person building various apps, websites, and tools. This policy covers everything I make. Because my projects are always evolving, I haven't fully decided how every bit of data will be used for every single thing, but I promise to handle any information I collect responsibly.

## 2. What I Might Collect
Depending on which of my projects you're using, I might collect:
* **The Basics:** If a project requires a login, I’ll likely have your email or username.
* **Technical Logs:** My servers usually log basic information like IP addresses to ensure things are running smoothly and to prevent abuse.
* **Project Specifics:** Some tools might need specific data to actually function. I only collect what is necessary for that specific project at that time.

## 3. How I Handle Your Data
* **I Don't Sell Data:** I have zero interest in selling or trading your information. 
* **Minimal Sharing:** I only share data if a service I’m using (like a database or web host) needs it to function, or if I’m legally required to do so.
* **Security:** I use standard security tools to keep things safe. While I can't guarantee 100% perfection, I do my best to protect your information.

## 4. Cookies
I use cookies where they make sense—mostly just to keep you logged in or remember your settings. They aren't meant to follow you around the rest of the internet.

## 5. Your Rights
If you want to know what info I have or you want me to delete everything related to you across my projects, just reach out.
